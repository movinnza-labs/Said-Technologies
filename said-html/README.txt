SAID Technologies Pvt. Ltd. — Static HTML Website
==================================================

HOW TO OPEN
  Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser and every link works,
  even without a web server. An internet connection is only needed for the
  Google Fonts (Inter and Plus Jakarta Sans); without it, system fonts are used.

HOW TO PUT IT ONLINE
  Upload the whole folder (keeping its structure) to any web host:
  cPanel / File Manager, Netlify Drop (app.netlify.com/drop), GitHub Pages, etc.

FOLDER STRUCTURE
  index.html                      Home
  what-we-do/                     What We Do, 10 categories, 39 service pages
  industries/                     Industries + 9 industry pages
  education/                      Education, 7 categories, 36 solution pages
  who-we-are/  careers/  insights/  contact/
  privacy-policy/  terms-of-use/  (DRAFT — not approved legal text)
  404.html                        Page-not-found page
  assets/site.css                 All styles
  assets/islands.js               Menus and forms (interactive parts)
  sitemap.xml, robots.txt

IMPORTANT
  - The enquiry and career forms are demonstrations: they validate input but
    do NOT send anything. Connect a form service before going live.
  - The text wordmark is temporary until the SAID logo is supplied.
  - Pictures are labelled placeholders until photographs are supplied.
  - These files are generated from the Next.js source project. For ongoing
    edits, change the source (content/ folder) and regenerate, rather than
    editing 115 HTML files by hand.
