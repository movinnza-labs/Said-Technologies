Scope of this policy

This policy describes how SAID Technologies Pvt. Ltd. handles personal information submitted through this website, including enquiry forms and career applications.

Information you provide

When you submit an enquiry, you may provide your name, organisation or institution, designation, email address, phone number, city or project location, area of interest, requirement details and preferred contact method.

When you share a career profile, you may also provide your current location, role of interest, experience, a resume and a message.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to your enquiry or application, to understand your requirement, and to contact you through your preferred method. It is not sold.

Retention, security and your choices

Details on retention periods, security safeguards, processors, cross-border transfers and how to access, correct or withdraw consent for your information will be confirmed in the approved policy.
