A Clear Scope for Every Engagement

A well-defined technology engagement should establish:

  • The business requirement and intended users.
  • Existing infrastructure and integration dependencies.
  • Equipment, software and licensing requirements.
  • Implementation and acceptance responsibilities.
  • Training, documentation and support coverage.
  • Recurring costs, renewals and exclusions.
