CoE Planning Framework

A Centre of Excellence should connect technology facilities with a clear academic purpose.

  • Academic objectives and target learners.
  • Technology and equipment scope.
  • Computing and software.
  • Site readiness and safety.
  • Faculty and student training.
  • Projects and competency assessment.
  • Maintenance and technical support.
  • Licensing and recurring expenditure.
